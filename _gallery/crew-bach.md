<p>This work uses combinations of eight colours taken two at a time. In mathematical terms, eight distinct colours give 28 unordered pairs when repetition is excluded. The catalogue connects CREW’s method to Bach’s contrapuntal practice: musical themes can be inverted, exchanged between voices and combined under precise constraints. The artwork translates the principle of composing within a rule into coloured paper elements. It does not transcribe a particular score; its connection to Bach lies in the relationship between a limited set of materials, their possible combinations and the structure of the completed composition.</p>
<p>CREW, 2023. Catalogue reference: CREW 470, page 21.</p>
