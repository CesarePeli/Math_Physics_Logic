<p>The catalogue identifies the rule of this coloured modular composition as the Queneau number Q14. Its grid belongs to CREW’s broader exploration of mathematical constraints: a chosen rule governs the organisation of discrete elements, while colour and physical construction make the resulting structure visible. The catalogue places this approach in the tradition of Oulipo, the literary workshop founded by Raymond Queneau and François Le Lionnais. In that tradition, formal restrictions generate possibilities for composition. Here the relationship between the coloured units and the whole can be read as a visual counterpart to writing organised by a numerical scheme. The catalogue does not provide a cell-by-cell decoding of this work.</p>
