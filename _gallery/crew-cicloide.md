<p>The catalogue associates this paper sculpture with two mathematical structures: a cycloid curve and the Collatz sequence starting at 40. A cycloid is the path traced by a point on a circle rolling along a straight line. The arithmetic sequence 40, 20, 10, 5, 16, 8, 4, 2, 1 supplies a second constraint. CREW brings geometry and numerical iteration into a construction that occupies space. This follows the catalogue’s account of the transition from flat compositions to sculptures: paper is lifted and bent so that mathematical organisation becomes volume, shadow and a form viewed from different directions. The catalogue names both rules without specifying their complete construction map.</p>
<p>CREW, 2023. Catalogue reference: CREW 110, page 49.</p>
