<p>This staircase-shaped sculpture is organised by the first Queneau numbers. Its title links Arnaut Daniel, Dante and Raymond Queneau. The catalogue follows that connection through the sestina: six end-words change position according to a fixed permutation across six stanzas. Arnaut’s poetic invention and Dante’s encounter with him in Purgatorio provide a literary background to Queneau’s later exploration of formal constraints. The staircase turns that connection into a physical object. The catalogue interprets climbing as an image of the effort involved in accepting a rule and working within it: the constraint provides the structure from which the artwork develops.</p>
<p>CREW, 2024. Catalogue reference: CREW 213, page 47.</p>
