<p>The coloured structure follows the Collatz sequence starting at 152. The rule divides an even number by two and replaces an odd number with three times that number plus one; the procedure is then repeated. From 152, the sequence reaches the cycle 4, 2, 1. CREW uses this arithmetic process as a constraint for a physical composition, making an iterative numerical structure visible through coloured strips. The catalogue presents the sequence in its section ‘Carta, Colla, Collatz’. The claim that every positive starting value eventually reaches 1 is the Collatz conjecture, not a proven theorem; reaching 1 in this particular case does not establish it for all numbers.</p>
<p>CREW, 2024. Catalogue reference: CREW 210, page 33.</p>
