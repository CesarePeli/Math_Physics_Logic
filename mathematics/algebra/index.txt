# Algebra

**Algebra** studies mathematical relations through symbols, equations, functions, and number systems.

It provides a language for expressing patterns, transforming problems, and describing relationships that extend far beyond elementary calculation. From solving equations to working with complex numbers, algebra connects concrete techniques with more abstract mathematical structures.

This section brings together resources from different levels of study, organized by mathematical topic rather than by school or university classification.

## Complex Numbers

Complex numbers extend the real number system by introducing the imaginary unit and reveal a richer algebraic and geometric structure.

They play a fundamental role in algebra, calculus, differential equations, physics, and many areas of higher mathematics.

## Why Algebra Matters

Algebra is not merely a collection of symbolic techniques. It is a way of representing structure.

Equations describe constraints, functions describe relationships, and algebraic transformations allow the same mathematical object to be viewed from different perspectives.

This flexibility makes algebra one of the central languages connecting elementary mathematics with calculus, geometry, physics, and more advanced mathematical theories.
