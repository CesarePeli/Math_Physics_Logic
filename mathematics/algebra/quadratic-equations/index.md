# Quadratic Equations

**Quadratic equations** are a fundamental part of algebra, connecting symbolic manipulation, factorisation, roots, discriminants, and the geometry of parabolas.

The resources collected here introduce quadratic equations step by step, from their standard form and basic types to factoring methods, completing the square, the general formula, and the analysis of the discriminant.
